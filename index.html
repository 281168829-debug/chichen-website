About Tianjin Chichen Trading

Based in the industrial hub of Tianjin, Tianjin Chichen Trading Co., Ltd. is a premier global supplier of high-performance cordless power tools. We serve distributors, wholesalers and e-commerce sellers across the United States and Europe with products that meet rigorous quality standards.

Our products are CE, RoHS, and UL certified, manufactured in ISO 9001-compliant facilities. We focus on the ONE 21V battery platform — a single battery that powers 60+ tools across 15 categories, reducing inventory complexity for our partners.

Every CHICHEN tool undergoes a full-load stress test before packaging. We stand behind our products with responsive after-sales support and a commitment to continuous improvement.
